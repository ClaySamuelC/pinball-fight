HOW TO PLAY

  • Hit targets to summon fighters into the lane.
  • The lane fights on its own. Destroy 3 towers, then the core.
  • Drop the ball and you wait 6 seconds. Your combo clears.
  • Combos multiply summons and zone buffs.
  • Orbs give Surge, Nuke, or a Turret.
  • Rush speeds your fighters. Aegis grants shields. Fury raises damage.
  • Destroy a tower and your table gains a bonus. The defender gets orbs.

Left flipper: A or Left. Right flipper: L or Right. Launch: hold Space.
